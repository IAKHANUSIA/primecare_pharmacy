Services / Home Health Care

Home Health Care

In-home care services can help someone who is ageing and needs assistance to live independently; is managing chronic health issues; is recovering from a medical setback; or has special needs or a disability. It can enable safety, security, and increased independence; it can ease management of an ongoing medical condition; it can help avoid unnecessary hospitalization; it can aid with recovery after an illness, injury, or hospital stay all through care given in the comfort and familiarity of home.

Live independently in the comfort of your home without compromising your health with the right kind of home health care.

Home health care is a wide range of health care services that can be given in your home for an illness or injury. It is usually less expensive, more convenient, and just as effective as care you get in a hospital or skilled nursing facility. Some research suggests nearly 90% of people over 65 want to age in place to live at home as long as possible. But to make it happen, additional care is often necessary, which is why some families turn to home health care services. It is all about trust. It is about feeling comfortable and, possibly, assisting you with the most intimate care.

If you or your loved one is looking for this home care, we at Primecare Pharmacy can help you. We understand the importance of personal independence and highly support. We offer caring home care services to you if you require extra support to live independently. Our healthcare professionals are experienced and highly trained to understand all your needs and offer the right kind of support whenever you need it.

Our services include but aren't limited to the following:
› 24-hour care
› Adult home care services
› Live-in care services
› Personal care (on a contracted hourly basis)
› Personal care assessments
› Risk assessments in your home
› Palliative care
› Personal hygiene care
› Transportation service
› Pre- and post-operative care

Live worry-free in your own home and don't rely on care facilities. Let us care for you.

We help you get the specialist homecare that you need at the best prices in town. Contact us today to know more about our home health care services.
