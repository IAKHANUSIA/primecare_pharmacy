Services / Weight Management

Weight Management

Weight management refers to a set of practices and behaviors that are necessary to keep one's weight at a healthful level. The purpose of weight management is to help each person to achieve and stay at the best weight possible in the context of overall health, occupation, and living situation.

Get in your best shape safely and efficiently with individualized weight management programs.

Healthy bodies come in many shapes and sizes. A healthy weight is one that contributes to your overall health, wellbeing, and quality of life. It is different for each person. A healthy weight is a weight that lowers your risk for health problems. But reaching a healthy weight isn't just about reaching a certain number on the scale or a certain BMI. Having healthy eating and exercise habits is very important.

Being underweight can represent as many health concerns to an individual as being overweight can. If a person is underweight, their body may not be getting the nutrients it needs to build healthy bones, skin, and hair.

Primecare Pharmacy can help you easily avoid such risks by helping you manage your weight in a safe environment. We focus on a healthy lifestyle, combining healthy foods and exercise. We offer customized weight management solutions to you based on your expectations, lifestyle, age, fitness levels, among other factors. We are a team of experienced professionals that can guide you through the process of weight management.

You can expect the following from us:
› Comprehensive assessment of your health and lifestyle
› Clinical diagnosis of your health as applicable
› Dietary assessment
› Psychological and social assessment as required
› Specialist advice and education on diet and lifestyle modifications
› Medical or surgical suggestions if required

Don't let your weight get in the way of your life. Let us help you get in the right shape so you can lead a healthy life.

We offer specific, measurable, achievable, realistic, and timely weight management solutions in the city at the best prices. Reach out to us now to know more about our weight management services.
