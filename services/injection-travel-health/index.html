Injection / Travel Health

Injections, also known as shots, deliver liquid medications, fluids, or nutrients directly into a person's body. A healthcare professional can use injections to administer vaccines and other types of medications into a person's vein, muscle, skin, or bone. Several different types of injections exist, depending on the purpose of the medication or substance in the shot.

Immunization has been a great public health success story!!

Vaccines protect travelers from serious diseases. Depending on where you travel, you may come into contact with diseases that are rare. Some vaccines may also be required for you to travel to certain places.

Getting vaccinated will help keep you safe and healthy while you're traveling. It will also help make sure that you don't bring any serious diseases home to your family, friends, and community.

At Primecare Pharmacy, a specialist vaccine clinic, you'll receive expert advice tailored to your itinerary and medical needs. We cover vaccines such as Yellow Fever, Malaria, Rabies, Typhoid, Hepatitis A, Hepatitis B, and many more.

What travel vaccinations do you need for my holiday?

The specific travel injections you will need depend on such factors as:

› Your age and health
› Which regions you are visiting
› What activities you will be doing

All you have to do is explain how your trip will look like and share your itinerary to receive your customized set of medications and vaccinations from our pharmacy based on your trip. We can provide both medical advice and administer the necessary medication to travelers in one quick stop.

Don't let your health issues get in the way of enjoying your vacation or work-related trip. Speak to us to start customizing your travel injections so that you can enjoy your trip while being healthy and staying comfortable throughout.
