Asthma/copd Management

Asthma and COPD (Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease) are obstructive pulmonary diseases that affect millions of people all over the world. Asthma can be diagnosed at any time in one's life. Having COPD for a long time may change the way your lungs work. Early diagnosis can be crucial to preserving lung function in people.

Learn how to deal with asthma or COPD under the guidance of expert healthcare professionals and pharmacists.

Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) is a collection of lung diseases that cause breathing problems and obstruct airflow. Asthma is considered severe when it is difficult to treat and manage the symptoms. Resources are available to help you ask the right questions about asthma management and treatment. Both COPD and asthma respond well to treatments like quitting smoking and airway-opening medications like bronchodilators.

Today asthma and COPD are not fully curable, not identified enough and not treated enough and the therapy is still developing. If you want to control your asthma or COPD, Primecare Pharmacy can help you with our asthma and COPD management services. No matter how big or small your symptoms are, we provide you with all the right support.

You can expect the following from our asthma and COPD management:
› Smoking cessation support
› Guidance on using inhalers, nebulizers, and tablets
› Pulmonary rehabilitation, a specialized program of exercise and education
› Non-invasive ventilation (NIV) treatment
› Surgery or lung transplantation
› High-end medication and long-term invasive procedure guidance and support

Don't let asthma or COPD hamper your lifestyle, and control their progression and symptoms. Let us help you.

We understand that managing asthma or COPD is not just about filling a prescription. So, we check in with you to ensure you have all the information and resources you need to feel confident and in-control of your condition. Contact Primecare Pharmacy to know more.
