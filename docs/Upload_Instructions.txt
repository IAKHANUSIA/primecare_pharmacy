     e:\Primecara Leduc\docs\pharmacy_license_windermere.pdf

   - Leduc Location (Permit #14544 - Sakibbhai Khanusia):

   - St. Albert Location (Permit #15544 - Chirag Patel):
     e:\Primecara Leduc\docs\pharmacy_license_stalbert.pdf

2. Alternatively, open your website license viewer page:
   e:\Primecara Leduc\docs\pharmacy_license.html?loc=1 (or ?loc=2, ?loc=3)
   Click "Admin Area: Attach / Upload College Approved Letter" (Passcode: admin),
   select the tab for the store location, choose your PDF file, and click Upload!

3. Each location's "Pharmacy License" button automatically opens its specific license document.
